# Setting

Part of the [game design](game_design.md).

For centuries this civilization has perfected fire, steam, and metal, until machines and the power of the elements are held with a belief close to religion. Electricity, and any machine that can think, is forbidden. Those lines of work have appeared only a few times, and the people responsible were removed before they could pass the knowledge on. The heroes are the best of the professions judged most able to end the peril and restore what was built. They now meet large machines driven by batteries and turbines, machines that produce amounts of energy their doctrine treats as unholy and that give motion to living-dead robotic abominations.
