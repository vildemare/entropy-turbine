# Mage

Part of the [player cast](../player.md). Designed, not in the current build. Build this after the [Gunslinger](gunslinger.md) slice stays readable.

The Mage is the short-range character. He carries fuel tanks and conjures from them. Fire is the element to build first. Other elements can come later as variations of the same tanks and the same cast, not as new characters. Do not add a second element until fire has been played.

## Look

Blue robes, long enough to read from the high camera. The tanks sit on the body, with brass caps and hoses so they belong to the soot-and-brass world rather than to a separate fantasy costume. Blue is the silhouette: he should not read as another brown coat beside the Gunslinger's stand-in or the enemy gunners.

## Fire

A cast does not send a shot across the route. It lays a burning patch a short distance along the aim direction. If the cursor is far away, the patch still stops at the weapon's maximum distance. Start playtests near 8 metres of placement and a patch a couple of metres across. That is far enough to reach a gap beside a barricade and short enough that the Mage has to step toward the fight. The Gunslinger's plasma still owns the long direct shot.

Enemies standing in the patch take damage over time. The patch lingers, so it denies the ground it covers: a lane, the mouth of a barricade, or the space a squad is trying to leave. That lingering hazard is the area denial. A patch under a tight group, or several patches, is the area destruction. The patch does not stop bullets. [Cover](../combat.md) still does that. Fire threatens bodies, not shots.

Enemies have no reaction to ground hazards yet. The first playable version can let them take the damage if they stand in it. Walking around a patch comes after the patch itself is readable.

Each cast spends fuel. Fuel is this character's ammunition. It is not a second health bar and not a pool other characters use. Holding the fire button repeats the cast at a limited rate and slows movement, the same two-thirds speed cost as the other characters. An empty tank does not cast. Continuing from a checkpoint refills the tanks, the same way the Gunslinger's drums are filled.

## Shop

The Mage uses the same [shop](../ui.md) frame, maximum-health row, and price ladder as the others. The first weapon rows to try are fuel capacity and how long a patch lasts. Radius waits until duration has been felt.
